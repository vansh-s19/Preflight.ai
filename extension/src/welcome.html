What it checks

Indian ID and bank details, phone numbers and emails, passwords and API keys, plus PDF, Word and text attachments.

Stays on your device

Every check runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to Preflight or anyone else.

You decide

A warning never blocks you. Swap in safe placeholders, send anyway, or go back and edit.

Pin it to your toolbar

  1. Click the puzzle-piece icon at the top right of Chrome.
  2. Click the pin next to Preflight.ai.
  3. Click the Preflight icon to pause it, switch it off for a site, or choose what it checks.
