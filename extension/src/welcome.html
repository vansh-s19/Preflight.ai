Preflight.ai is on

It checks what you're about to send to ChatGPT and Claude, and warns you before personal data or secrets go out.

What it checks

Aadhaar, PAN, GSTIN and bank details, phone numbers and emails, API keys, passwords, and PDF, Word and text attachments.

Stays on your device

Every check runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to Preflight or anyone else.

You decide

A warning never blocks you. Replace the data with safe placeholders, send anyway, or go back and edit.

Try it

Copy this made-up example, paste it into a chat and press send. Preflight will stop it before it leaves.

My PAN is ABCDE1234F, call me on +91 9876543210
Open ChatGPT Open Claude

Keep it in reach

  1. Click the puzzle-piece icon at the top right of Chrome.
  2. Click the pin next to Preflight.ai.
  3. Click the Preflight icon any time to pause it, turn it off for a site, or choose what it checks.

Preflight is a safety net, not a guarantee. It catches the common mistakes, so still take a look before you paste. Privacy policy
